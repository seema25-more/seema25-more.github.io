### Overview
Developed a Power BI dashboard to monitor sales performance and key business metrics. The dashboard enables stakeholders to track trends, compare performance across periods, and identify growth opportunities.

### Key Insights
- Analyzed sales trends across time periods and regions.
- Identified top-performing products and revenue drivers.
- Tracked KPIs such as total sales, growth rate, and category-wise performance.
- Enabled data-driven decision-making through interactive visualizations.

### Tools & Technologies
Power BI, Data Modeling, DAX, Business Analytics
